## Company Signals

**Values to reflect:**
- Speed ("Hello to Hired in 48 hours") — show you work fast and ship
- AI-first thinking — not "we add AI to features," but "we design for AI as the primary actor"
- Candidate empathy — their product must reduce hiring anxiety, not add to it

**Vocabulary to use:**
- "candidate experience" (their end user)
- "hiring velocity" (their value prop to enterprise clients)
- "AI-native" (how they position themselves)
- "match intelligence" (their core AI capability)

**Avoid:**
- Treating this like a traditional enterprise tool design role — it has a strong consumer/human-anxiety dimension
- Vague answers about "user-centred design" without numbers

**Questions to ask them:**
1. *"Octo handles the first screening — how is the design team involved in improving Octo's candidate-facing UX? Is that within scope for this role?"*
2. *"What's the biggest UX challenge you're seeing in the candidate experience right now — pre-interview, during, or post?"*
3. *"The role is onsite — what does the design workflow look like? Are designers embedded in product squads or centralized?"*
