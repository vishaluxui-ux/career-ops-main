## Proof Point: AI Integration at Bugasura

**Company:** Bugasura (Moolya Software Testing Pvt. Ltd.)
**Period:** Oct 2020 – April 2026

**What was built:** Integrated AI into the core Bugasura workflows — requirement writing, test case generation (Testpert), and issue creation. Users could complete these tasks 2x faster with AI assistance.

**Key metrics:**
- 2x faster: writing requirements, test cases, and issues with AI
- Testpert: saved 10 hours/week in manual testing effort (automated test case generation from requirements)
- 50,000+ users benefited from simplified, AI-augmented workflows
- Bug reporting time cut to under 10 seconds (Chrome Extension + in-app Widget)
- 3x increase in bug reporting frequency after AI workflow redesign

**When to use:** Any role involving AI product design, AI workflow UX, intelligent automation, or B2B SaaS with AI features.
